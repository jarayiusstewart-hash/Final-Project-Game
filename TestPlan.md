# Hollowlight — Vertical Slice Test Plan

**Build:** Alpha Vertical Slice  
**Platform:** WebGL on itch.io  
**Testing Session:** Week of November 9, 2026  
**Developer:** Solo

## 1. Testing Goal

Determine whether first-time players can understand the guiding flame mechanic, navigate the branching cave, reach the hidden objective, and restart the game without assistance.

## 2. Before the Test — Tester Script

Tell each tester:

"You're about to play Hollowlight, a game where a mysterious flame guides you through a dark cave. Please play using only the instructions provided in the game. I'll observe without giving hints. Afterward, I'll ask you a few questions about your experience."

Do not explain where the objective is or which corridor to follow.

## 3. Tasks for Testers

1. Read the on-screen controls and begin playing.
2. Use the guiding flame to navigate the cave.
3. Find and reach the hidden objective.
4. Restart the game after completing the objective.

Allow approximately 5–10 minutes per tester.

## 4. Post-Play Questions and Measurements

| Question | What to Observe or Measure |
|---|---|
| 1. How clear were the movement and flame controls? | Rate clarity from 1–5; record whether the tester needed help. |
| 2. Did you understand where the flame wanted you to go? | Record wrong turns, hesitation, and whether the tester followed the flame. |
| 3. Was the cave too dark, too bright, or appropriately lit? | Record a 1–5 visibility rating and locations
